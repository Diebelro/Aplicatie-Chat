Diebel Chat helps you meet people, message in real time, and make voice or video calls, all in one app.

You can create your profile and discover people nearby, send text messages and share photos or files, start audio or video calls with one tap, see who is online, manage your privacy settings, and block or report users when you need to.

Diebel is designed for adults who want a simple, fast way to connect. Messages sync quickly, calls use secure connections, and the interface works on phones and tablets.

You choose what to share on your profile. Location is optional and can be turned off. You can delete your account and data anytime from Settings. Privacy policy: https://chat.diebel.ro/privacy

Core features are free. No ads in the app.

You need an internet connection, a microphone for voice calls, and a camera for video calls. Users must be 18 or older.
