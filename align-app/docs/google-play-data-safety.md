# Google Play Data Safety - Diebel

Use this as the internal reference when completing Google Play Console forms for Internal Testing. Keep the answers aligned with the public Privacy Policy at:

`https://chat.diebel.ro/privacy`

## Core Statements

- Do not claim that no data is collected.
- Do not claim that Diebel never has access to messages.
- Do not claim end-to-end encryption unless the product is changed to provide it.
- Do not claim message content is used for ads, profiling, or analytics.
- Do not claim data is sold.

## Data Handling Position

- Data is processed for app functionality: account access, profile, discovery, chat, calls, notifications, security, technical maintenance, and legal compliance.
- Only the minimum data required for these functions should be processed.
- Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS between the app and servers.
- Users can request account/data deletion by contacting `contact@diebel.ro`.
- Personal data is not sold.
- Personal data is not shared with third parties for their own advertising or profiling.
- Service providers may process data only as needed to provide hosting, storage, email, push notifications, security, and similar operational services.

## Messages

- Messages are not routinely read or analysed.
- Message content may be accessed only when strictly necessary for technical maintenance, security, abuse investigation, or legal compliance.
- Message content is not used for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

## Likely Google Play Data Categories

- Personal info: email, name/profile details, age/date of birth, gender, optional profile information.
- Photos and videos: profile photos and chat attachments if users upload them.
- Messages: text messages and permitted attachments sent through chat.
- Location: only if the user grants permission or enables location-related features.
- App activity: matches, visits, settings, reports, blocks, and similar in-app actions needed for functionality and safety.
- App info and performance / device identifiers: technical logs, device/browser/network data, push tokens, and diagnostics needed for operation, security, and notifications.
- Audio/video access: camera and microphone are used only during active calls and calls are not recorded as stored audio/video files by the service.

## Recommended Play Console Notes

- Purpose: app functionality, security, fraud/abuse prevention, account management, user communications, and legal compliance.
- Sharing: no sale; no third-party sharing for third-party ads/profiling. Operational processors may handle data under service-provider roles.
- Deletion: users can request deletion via `contact@diebel.ro`; app/account deletion flows should remain aligned with this statement.
